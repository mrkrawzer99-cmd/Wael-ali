About Sudan

Learn about the history, people, and background of the Republic of Sudan

History of Sudan

Sudan has one of the oldest and most interesting histories in the whole world. Long before modern Sudan existed, this land was home to some of the most powerful kingdoms in ancient Africa. The Nubian civilization, which started along the Nile River, was one of the first great civilizations in human history.

Ancient Kingdoms

The Kingdom of Kush was one of the most powerful kingdoms in ancient Africa. It existed from around 1070 BC to 350 AD. The people of Kush built pyramids, temples, and cities. At one point, the Kushite kings even ruled over Egypt! They were called the "Black Pharaohs" and they are a very important part of African history.

After the Kingdom of Kush, another great kingdom called the Kingdom of Meroe came. This kingdom was also very powerful and built many pyramids. Today you can visit these pyramids in northern Sudan near the city of Shendi.

Sudan During the Islamic Period

Islam came to Sudan around the 7th century. Over time, many of the people in Sudan converted to Islam. Several Islamic kingdoms were formed, such as the Funj Sultanate, which was a very important kingdom that lasted from 1504 to 1821. During this time, the city of Sennar became an important center of Islamic learning and trade.

Colonial Period and Independence

In the 1800s, Sudan was first ruled by the Egyptian Ottoman Empire, and later by the British and Egyptian together. This period is called the Anglo-Egyptian Condominium (1899-1956). During this time, the British built many roads, schools, and government buildings in Sudan.

Sudan gained its independence on January 1, 1956. This was a very important moment for the Sudanese people. After independence, Sudan became a member of the Arab League and the United Nations.

The People of Sudan

Sudan has a very diverse population. There are more than 500 different ethnic groups in Sudan. The largest groups are Arabs, Nubians, Beja, Nuer, and Fur. Most people in Sudan speak Arabic, but there are also many local languages spoken in different regions of the country.
