About Sudan

Sudan is a country located in northeastern Africa. It is officially known as the Republic of Sudan. Sudan has a long and rich history that goes back thousands of years. The country borders Egypt to the north, Libya to the northwest, Chad to the west, the Central African Republic to the southwest, South Sudan to the south, Ethiopia to the southeast, and Eritrea to the east.

The capital city of Sudan is Khartoum, which is located where the Blue Nile and White Nile rivers meet. This makes Khartoum one of the most important cities in Africa. Sudan has a population of about 45 million people and covers a very large area of land.

Why Learn About Sudan?

Sudan is a very special country. It has more ancient pyramids than Egypt! It also has the famous Nile River passing through it, which makes the land very fertile. The people of Sudan are known for being friendly and hospitable. The culture of Sudan is very rich and includes music, traditional clothes, and delicious food.

Here are some reasons why Sudan is an interesting place to study:

  • Sudan has more than 200 Nubian pyramids
  • It is one of the largest countries in Africa
  • The Nile River flows through Sudan for more than 3,000 km
  • Sudan has a very diverse culture with over 500 different ethnic groups
  • The ancient Nubian civilization was one of the oldest in the world

Did You Know?

Sudan has more ancient pyramids than Egypt! There are about 255 known pyramids in Sudan, compared to 138 in Egypt. These pyramids were built by the rulers of the Nubian kingdoms of Kushite and Meroitic periods.
