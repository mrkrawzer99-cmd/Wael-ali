Questions and Answers About Sudan

Here you will find answers to some of the most common questions people ask about Sudan. If your question is not answered here, please visit our Contact page to send us a message.

General Questions

Q1. Where is Sudan located?
Sudan is located in northeastern Africa. It is bordered by Egypt to the north, Libya to the northwest, Chad to the west, the Central African Republic to the southwest, South Sudan to the south, Ethiopia to the southeast, and Eritrea to the east. It also has a coastline on the Red Sea to the northeast.
Q2. What is the capital city of Sudan?
The capital city of Sudan is Khartoum. It is located at the point where the Blue Nile and White Nile rivers join together. Khartoum is the largest city in Sudan and the center of government, business, and culture.
Q3. What language do people speak in Sudan?
The official languages of Sudan are Arabic and English. Arabic is the most widely spoken language. However, Sudan has a very diverse population and there are more than 100 different languages spoken by different ethnic groups throughout the country.
Q4. What is the religion of Sudan?
The majority of people in Sudan are Muslim. Islam has been present in Sudan since the 7th century and plays an important role in daily life, culture, and law. There are also small communities of Christians and people who follow traditional African religions.

History Questions

Q5. When did Sudan become independent?
Sudan gained its independence on January 1, 1956. Before that, Sudan was ruled jointly by Britain and Egypt, a period called the Anglo-Egyptian Condominium. Sudan was one of the first African countries to gain independence from colonial rule.
Q6. Does Sudan really have more pyramids than Egypt?
Yes, this is true! Sudan has more ancient pyramids than Egypt. There are about 255 known pyramids in Sudan, compared to around 138 in Egypt. The Sudanese pyramids were built by the rulers of the ancient Nubian kingdoms of Kush and Meroe. They are smaller than Egyptian pyramids but there are more of them!
Q7. What was the Kingdom of Kush?
The Kingdom of Kush was a powerful ancient kingdom in what is now northern Sudan. It existed from around 2000 BC to 350 AD. The Kushite people built pyramids and temples and were very advanced. At one point, Kushite kings conquered Egypt and ruled as pharaohs. These rulers are sometimes called the "Black Pharaohs" of ancient history.

Travel and Tourism Questions

Q8. What are the best places to visit in Sudan?
Some of the most popular places to visit in Sudan include:
  • Meroe Pyramids - ancient Nubian pyramids in the desert
  • Khartoum - the capital city with museums and the confluence of the Nile
  • The Red Sea Coast - beautiful beaches and coral reefs
  • Dinder National Park - wildlife and nature
  • Nubian villages in the north - beautiful traditional architecture
Q9. What is the best time to visit Sudan?
The best time to visit Sudan is between November and February. During these months, the weather is cooler and more comfortable for tourists. The summer months (June to September) are very hot, especially in the desert areas of northern Sudan. During the rainy season (July to September), some roads can become difficult to travel on.

Culture Questions

Q10. What is traditional Sudanese food like?
Sudanese food is very flavorful and filling. The most common dishes include ful medames (cooked fava beans), asida (sorghum porridge), kisra (flat bread), and various meat stews. Sudanese people are known for their hospitality and always offer food and tea to guests. Sudanese coffee and tea ceremonies are also an important part of the culture.
